Turn 2023 training-program records into decision-ready comparisons.

What could be improved

Questions and recommendations for further investigation.

These are practical next steps informed by the patterns shown here. They are not findings or causal conclusions proven by this dataset.

Question

Investigate lower totals in later terms.

Compare the recorded decline in trainee totals with scheduling, registration, operational, and reporting context that is not included in the workbook.

Recommendation

Review high-volume program delivery.

Before allocating resources, review the staffing, scheduling, availability, and delivery conditions of program titles with high reported participation.

Recommendation

Compare capacity with participation.

Use separately held capacity and enrolment data alongside available course counts before inferring demand, performance, or service quality.

Recommendation

Strengthen future data collection.

Collect attendance, completion, satisfaction, target audience, and cancellation-reason fields to support more complete future analysis.

Source & method

Local Saudi Open Data / SDAIA training-program data

Primary source: xlsx file.xlsx, a local Excel workbook containing the Institute of Information Technology’s detailed 2023 training-program records. It has four source sheets: الفصل الأول 2023, الفصل الثاني 2023, الفصل الثالث 2023, and الفصل الرابع 2023.

The dashboard contains a faithful normalized in-browser representation of the workbook’s detail rows, so it opens directly without an upload, network request, or server. csv file.csv is a parity export and is not added again. Blank source cells remain missing: one course-count value is excluded from available-course totals, and most training locations are not recorded. Trainee values are reported counts on program records, not verified unique people, attendance, completion, satisfaction, or outcomes.
